How to read this. Under the price, the top strip is the model's call each day (teal = BUY, red = SELL). The strip below it shows whether that call turned out right a month later: green = right, red = wrong, grey = too recent to tell. Every call to the left of today was made by a network trained before those days, so you are seeing real out-of-sample results, not a replay of what it had already learned. "Right" means: SELL before a drop of 8% or more within about a month, or BUY when no such drop followed. This is research output, not financial advice.
