What the monthly service includes

One workflow is set up around the client’s research routine. The monthly handoff says what changed, when it was checked, and which sources support it.

Personal setup

Agree on one primary company, up to three related public tickers, and the questions or thesis points that matter to the client.

Ongoing evidence view

When SEC and news providers are configured, MIRA checks saved research for new filings and headlines about every six hours while its host is awake. It keeps source links and timestamps for review.

Monthly handoff

Receive a plain-language summary of notable changes, supporting and conflicting evidence, data dates, uncertainties, and next questions to investigate.

How it works each month

The client gets a repeatable process with a human review of the output. The automated collectors gather material; they do not decide whether a stock will rise or fall.

Useful for

  • Keeping one client’s public-company research organized month to month
  • Tracking thesis assumptions beside dated filings and headlines
  • Seeing price history and descriptive calculations with clear as-of dates, when a permitted provider is available

What MIRA does not do

  • It does not learn forecast weights from each live headline; news and filings are evidence to review
  • It does not provide a validated next-session up/down call or a proven return edge
  • It does not send automatic alerts or run continuously when the host is asleep
  • It does not give BUY/SELL advice, access brokerage accounts, or execute trades
  • This prototype has no client login or billing portal; share public tickers/questions only, not holdings or confidential material
Prototype status: MIRA’s current forecasting model has not demonstrated useful performance over its simple baseline. The $50/month offer is for an organized, source-traceable research workflow with a monthly human-reviewed handoff—not for prediction accuracy. Provider terms must allow any news or market data used.
