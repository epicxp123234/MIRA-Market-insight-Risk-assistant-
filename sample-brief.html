What the source says

EvidenceReported factWhy it matters to the research question
ReportedRevenueQ3 FY2027 revenue was $115.8B, versus $57.0B in Q3 FY2026 and $96.2B in Q2 FY2027.Revenue rose sequentially and more than doubled year over year. This is company-reported revenue, not an estimate of future growth.
ReportedSegment mixData Center revenue was $107.6B. Dividing by total revenue gives approximately 92.9%.The business is highly exposed to Data Center demand; the percentage is a simple calculation from reported figures.
ReportedMarginsGAAP gross margin was 74.8%, down from 75.0% in Q2 FY2027, while above 73.4% in Q3 FY2026.Sequential movement and year-over-year movement differ. The next-quarter outlook is the key follow-up.
OutlookNext quarterManagement forecast $130B revenue, plus or minus 3%, and 71–72% GAAP gross margin. The outlook includes an additional week and assumes no Data Center compute revenue from China.Compare the outlook with realized results later, while accounting for the extra week and stated assumptions.
ReportedCustomer concentrationThree direct customers represented 14%, 11%, and 11% of Q3 revenue, or 36% combined.Direct customers include several types of intermediaries. They should not be treated as the final end customers.

Questions to investigate next

  • What is driving the expected sequential gross-margin decline, and which costs are temporary?
  • How does the new product ramp affect supply commitments, inventory, and customer delivery timing?
  • How stable is direct-customer concentration, and what visibility is available on end demand?
